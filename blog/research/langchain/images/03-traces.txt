A data visualization of "LangSmith Traces" showing the observability of a Deep Agent.

Visual elements:
1.  **Timeline View**: A horizontal timeline showing a long-running task (e.g., "1 hour duration").
2.  **Hierarchical Trace**: The timeline is broken down into nested bars.
    -   Top bar: "Main Agent Task".
    -   Middle bars: "Planner", "VFS Write", "Sub-agent: Researcher".
    -   Bottom bars: "Tool: Search", "Tool: Read File".
3.  **Status Indicators**: Green checks for success, one yellow warning sign handled by a "Retry" middleware loop.
4.  **Middleware Inspection**: A magnifying glass or callout box focusing on one step, showing "Middleware Interception: Summarization Triggered".

Style:
-   UI mockup style, similar to a modern SaaS dashboard (dark mode).
-   Clean lines, data-dense but readable layout.
-   Colors: Shades of green (success), yellow (warning), and slate grey (UI background).
-   Professional, analytics-focused aesthetic.
