# Anthropic Economic Index: New Building Blocks for Understanding AI Use
Date: Jan 15, 2026
Source: https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-primitives

## Overview

Anthropic's fourth Economic Index report introduces "economic primitives" - five foundational measurements to track AI's economic impact over time.

## The Five Economic Primitives

1. **Task Complexity**
   - Human time to complete tasks without AI
   - Time spent completing tasks with AI
   - Multiple tasks within single conversations

2. **Human and AI Skills**
   - Whether users could complete tasks without Claude
   - Years of education needed to understand prompts and responses
   - Skill-biased technical change implications

3. **Use Case**
   - Professional use
   - Educational use
   - Personal use

4. **AI Autonomy**
   - Degree of decision-making delegation
   - Spectrum from collaboration to full delegation

5. **Task Success**
   - Claude's assessment of task completion
   - Feasibility and cost of automation

## Key Findings from November 2025 Data

### Task-Level Insights

- **Complexity and Speedup**: More complex tasks see greater speedup
  - High school education tasks (12 years): 9x speedup
  - College degree tasks (16 years): 12x speedup
  - Success rates: 66% (college) vs 70% (less than high school)

- **Time Horizons**:
  - Claude.ai: 50% success on 19-hour tasks
  - API: 50% success on 3.5-hour tasks
  - METR benchmark: 50% success on 2-hour tasks

- **Geographic Variation**:
  - Higher GDP countries: More work and personal use
  - Lower GDP countries: More educational use
  - Follows adoption curve pattern

### Occupation-Level Insights

- **Coverage**: 49% of jobs use Claude for at least 25% of tasks (up from 36%)
- **Skill Content**: Claude covers tasks requiring 14.4 years education (vs economy avg 13.2)
- **Net Effect**: Current usage pattern suggests potential deskilling of jobs
  - Removes higher-education tasks from occupations
  - Affects technical writers, travel agents, teachers
  - Dynamic labor market adjustments not captured

### Aggregate Economic Impact

- **Productivity Gains**:
  - Raw speedup estimate: 1.8 percentage points/year increase
  - Adjusted for reliability: 1.2 points (Claude.ai) to 1.0 points (API)
  - Would return US to late 1990s/early 2000s productivity growth rates

- **Context**: Does not account for:
  - Future model improvements
  - More sophisticated AI use patterns
  - Recent Claude Opus 4.5 release

## Evolution of Previous Measures

- **Task Concentration**: Top 10 tasks now 24% (up from 21% in Jan 2025)
- **Computer/Mathematical**: 33% of Claude.ai, 50% of API traffic
- **Augmentation vs Automation**:
  - Current: 52% augmentation, 45% automation (Claude.ai)
  - Trend: Slow rise in automation over 2025
- **Geographic Distribution**:
  - US concentration decreasing
  - Model predicts equalization across states in 2-5 years

## Methodology

- Sample: 1M conversations from Claude.ai + 1M from first-party API
- Privacy-preserving analysis
- Claude evaluates primitives for each conversation
- Links to O*NET occupational database

## Implications

1. **Uneven Impact**: AI use remains concentrated by country, occupation, task type
2. **Leading Indicator**: Primitives provide early signals of economic transformation
3. **Business Adoption Critical**: API vs Claude.ai migration indicates enterprise readiness
4. **Policy Foundation**: Empirical data for policy responses to technological transition
